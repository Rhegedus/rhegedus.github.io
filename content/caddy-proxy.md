### The Setup

A lightweight, highly resilient reverse-proxy layer configured to orchestrate incoming edge traffic, automate SSL termination, and securely forward requests to multi-tenant backends.

### Configuration Strategy

Leveraging Caddy's declarative configuration engine to minimize infrastructure footprint while maintaining strict request isolation and precise logging.
