### The Objective

Built an automated document-retrieval tool that logs into a user's insurance carrier portal (Lemonade, AAA) and downloads their policy documents automatically.

### Integration Highlights

* **Browser Automation:** Browserless.io/Puppeteer-driven browser automation controlling carrier portal sessions.
* **Redis-Backed State Machine:** Manages carrier-specific auth flows, including conditional MFA, as a durable state machine.
