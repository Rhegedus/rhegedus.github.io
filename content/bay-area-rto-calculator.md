### The Tool

An interactive, multi-factor cost calculator modeling the true cost of in-office work — commute (IRS mileage rate vs. fuel-only), tolls, parking, transit, childcare/petcare premiums, and time-opportunity cost, netted against WFH offset expenses.

### Purpose

Designed as both a standalone public tool and an entry point into operations-audit consulting work.
